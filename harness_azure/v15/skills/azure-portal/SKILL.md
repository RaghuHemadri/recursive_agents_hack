---
name: azure-portal
description: Fastest way to open any Azure service or blade
---
- Open services with the global search bar ("Search resources, services, and docs"): type the name, click the match under Services.
- List pages (#browse / #servicemenu) have a toolbar: "+ Create" opens the creation wizard (allowed; nothing is created).
- Slow blades show loading dots: wait, then observe. Breadcrumb goes back; X closes a blade.
- Open services via the left-hand navigation menu: locate the service under "Azure services" and click its name.
